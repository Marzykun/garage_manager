==============================================================
 GARAGE MANAGER — UPDATE INSTRUCTIONS
 For a phone that already has Garage Manager set up and working.
==============================================================

!! IMPORTANT: DO PART 1 (UPDATE THE SERVER) AND PART 3 (RESTART)
!! BEFORE USING THE NEW APP. If you install the app but skip the
!! server update, Reports and other screens show "HTTP 404 ...
!! Cannot GET /api/reports/..." errors.

WHAT THIS UPDATE ADDS
 - NEW BILL FORMATS. Jobs with tyres print a "Cash Bill" (like the
   paper tyre bill) with CGST 9% + SGST 9% added on the tyre amount.
   Jobs without tyres print a "LABOUR BILL" with NO GST. Labour on a
   tyre bill carries no GST.
 - Reports: "Payments by Mode" (how many bills paid by Cash / UPI /
   Card ...), and "Tyres Bought" (how many tyres were bought from
   the dealer, with cost)
 - Reports: the Live Overview cards now follow Today / 7 Days /
   30 Days
 - A job cannot be marked Completed until its invoice is generated
   (the app says "Invoice not generated" and offers Open Billing)
 - Adding a tyre or a part that already exists ADDS to the existing
   stock instead of making a second card
 - Add Tyre has optional Dealer and Cost price; the history icon on
   the Tyre Stock screen lists everything bought
 - Full Service log entry: pick parts from the inventory and they
   are deducted from stock. Removing the job puts them back
 - Service Breakdown no longer counts removed jobs
 - "Start Server" button on the login screen (turns the server on
   without opening Termux)
 - Print / share a proper invoice (with garage name, address,
   GSTIN, invoice number, GST breakup)
 - Automatic data backup every month, plus Back Up Now / Share /
   Restore in Settings
 - Customers tab now opens with the full customer list. Search by
   name or phone number, filter by vehicle type (Car / SUV / Bike /
   Auto / Commercial) and by service. Tap a customer to see their
   phone (Call / WhatsApp), vehicles, every visit and amount billed.
 - New Job Card asks for Vehicle Type and Make / Model (optional)
 - "Edit Details" on every job (the ... menu) — fix a wrong name,
   phone, number plate, vehicle or service without deleting the job
 - New Job: type a number plate seen before and the customer's name,
   phone and vehicle fill in by themselves, with their last 3 visits
   (tap the green box for full history). Kilometres box added.
 - Customers sorted A-Z (or by recent visit)
 - Tyre stock: Pattern / Design field, "No stock" shown in Log
   Details, and Compare Tyres (one size, all brands, price + stock)
 - Reports: tap Pending Dues or New Customers for the full list,
   Revenue by Category, Tyre Sales summary (day / week / month)
 - Fixed: tapping the status button on a job could make it vanish;
   jobs are now completed from ... > Mark as Completed
 - Removing a job puts its logged tyres back in stock

WHAT YOU NEED
 - The client's phone, connected to Wi-Fi (internet is needed for
   Part 1 only)
 - About 15 minutes
 - The garage's address, phone number and GSTIN (if they have one)

Do the parts IN ORDER. In Termux, type each line exactly and press
Enter after each one. Wait for the $ sign to come back before typing
the next line.

--------------------------------------------------------------
 PART 1 — UPDATE THE SERVER  (in Termux)
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the Termux app.
2. Type:

    cd ~/garage_manager
    git pull

   You should see a list of changed files, ending with something
   like "30 files changed". That is correct.

   If it says "Already up to date" — fine, continue.
   If it shows an ERROR or mentions "conflict" — STOP and send us
   a photo of the screen.

--------------------------------------------------------------
 PART 2 — ONE-TIME TERMUX SETTINGS  (in Termux)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Still in Termux, type these two lines. They let the app's
"Start Server" button work (safe to do even if done before):

    echo "allow-external-apps=true" >> ~/.termux/termux.properties
    termux-reload-settings

Then type this one. The phone will ask about access to files /
storage — tap ALLOW:

    termux-setup-storage

(This lets the monthly backups be saved in the phone's storage, so
they are safe even if Termux is ever removed.)

--------------------------------------------------------------
 PART 3 — RESTART THE PHONE
--------------------------------------------------------------
Restart the phone and wait about 1 minute after it turns on.
The server starts by itself and updates the database automatically.
(Nothing is deleted — all old jobs, customers and invoices stay.)

--------------------------------------------------------------
 PART 4 — INSTALL THE NEW APP
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. On the phone, open this link in the browser:

    https://github.com/Marzykun/garage_manager/releases/latest

2. Under "Assets", tap  app-release.apk  to download it.
   (Use ONLY this link — do not use any older app file you may
   have saved before.)
3. Open the downloaded file and tap Install / Update.

   If it says "App not installed" or "conflicts with an existing
   package":
     - Settings > Apps > Garage Manager > Uninstall
     - Then open app-release.apk again and Install.
     - This is safe: the garage data is kept in Termux, not in the
       app. You only need to log in again.

--------------------------------------------------------------
 PART 5 — FILL IN THE GARAGE DETAILS  (in the app)
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Garage Manager. Under the title it should say a green
   "Server online". (If red, tap "Start Server" and wait 10 sec.)
2. Log in as OWNER.
3. Go to Settings (bottom right).
4. In "Garage Info" fill in:
     - Garage Name
     - Address
     - Phone
     - GSTIN  (only if the garage is GST registered — otherwise
               leave it empty)
5. Tap "Save Garage Info".

These details are printed at the top of every invoice.

--------------------------------------------------------------
 PART 6 — CHECK EVERYTHING WORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------
BACKUP:
 1. Still in Settings, scroll down to "Data Backup".
 2. Tap "Back Up Now". A new backup should appear in the list.
 3. It should say  "Saved in phone storage → GarageManager/backups"
    in green. If it shows an orange warning about "inside Termux
    only", redo the  termux-setup-storage  line from Part 2 and
    make sure you tap Allow.

INVOICE:
 1. Open any job that already has an invoice (or create one).
 2. Tap "View & Print Invoice".
 3. Check the garage name, address, GSTIN and amounts look right.
 4. "Print Invoice" prints it (or choose "Save as PDF").
    "Share PDF" sends it on WhatsApp.

CHECK THE BILLS:
 - Make a tyre job: the bill should say "Cash Bill" with CGST 9% and
   SGST 9%. Make a service-only job: "LABOUR BILL", no GST.
 - (The GST % box in Settings is no longer used by the bills.)

CUSTOMERS + EDIT:
 1. Add a test job with New Job (pick a Vehicle Type).
 2. On that job tap ... > Edit Details, change the name, tap Save
    Changes. The job card should show the new name.
 3. Owner: open the Customers tab — the customer list appears
    straight away. Try typing part of a phone number, and tapping
    a vehicle type. Tap a customer to see their details.
 4. New Job again, type the same number plate — the green
    "Returning" box should appear and fill in the details.
 5. Remove the test jobs afterwards (... > Remove).

START SERVER BUTTON (optional test):
 - Only shows when the server is off. If you ever see a red
   "Server not responding" on the login screen, tap "Start Server"
   and wait about 10 seconds for it to turn green.
 - The first time, Android may ask permission — tap Allow.

--------------------------------------------------------------
 TELL THE OWNER
--------------------------------------------------------------
 - Data is backed up automatically every month.
 - Once a month: Settings > Data Backup > tap Share on the newest
   backup and send it to their own WhatsApp or Google Drive. That
   copy is what saves their data if the phone is lost or broken.
 - Before any big change, tap "Back Up Now".
 - When adding a job, pick the Vehicle Type — the Customers screen
   can only filter by type for vehicles where it was picked.
 - A typo in a name / phone / number plate: ... > Edit Details on
   the job. It fixes it everywhere, old invoices included.
 - To finish a job: ... > Mark as Completed. The coloured status
   label on the card is now just a label.
 - Add a tyre or part again with the same name and it adds to the
   old card. Cards that were already duplicated before this update
   stay as they are — delete the extra ones by hand.
 - Complete a job only after making its invoice.
 - Tyres taken out by jobs removed BEFORE this update were never
   put back. Check Tyre Stock counts once and correct them.

--------------------------------------------------------------
 IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------
 - App says server not responding even after "Start Server":
   restart the phone, wait 1 minute, try again.
 - Still not working: open Termux, type

    cat ~/garage-backend.log

   and send us a photo of the screen.
 - Login says  role "postgres" does not exist, or Postgres will not
   start ("Address already in use"): follow RESET_DATABASE_INSTRUCTIONS.txt
   (only after checking there is no real data on the phone).
 - "Start Server" gives a Termux error: check Part 2 was typed
   exactly, then restart the phone.
